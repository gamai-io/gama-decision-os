GAMA Decision OS — autonomni kontrolni nivo za agente GAMA Decision OS — Autonomous Agent Control Plane

Agenti sve više izvršavaju, a ne samo odgovaraju. GAMA Decision OS stoji između modela i vašeg ERP-a, baze i e-maila: procijeni rizik prije akcije, izoluje izvršenje u hardverski sandbox i vrati dokaz koji se može provjeriti. Bez lažnog „done“. Agents now execute, not just answer. GAMA Decision OS sits between the model and your ERP, database and email: it scores risk before the action, isolates execution in a hardware sandbox, and returns verifiable evidence. No fake “done”.

FAQ

Česta pitanjaCommon questions

Da li Decision OS zamjenjuje našeg agenta?Does Decision OS replace our agent?

Ne. To je kontrolni sloj: agent ostaje isti (OpenCode, Claude Code, LangGraph, vlastiti loop), a poziva L0 prije alata i Evidence Gate poslije. Integracija je jedan middleware poziv ili REST endpoint.No. It is a control layer: your agent stays the same (OpenCode, Claude Code, LangGraph, custom loop) and calls L0 before a tool and the Evidence Gate after. Integration is one middleware call or a REST endpoint.

Kako se dokazuje „0 false-success“?How is “0 false-success” proven?

Agentov `ok` se ne računa. Nezavisni pytest (fail-to-pass + pass-to-pass) odlučuje o uspjehu, a `tests/` se vraća na pristine prije provjere. Rezultat: 10/10 na SWE-bench-lite, 0 lažnih potvrda.The agent's `ok` is not counted. Independent pytest (fail-to-pass + pass-to-pass) decides success, and `tests/` is restored to pristine before verification. Result: 10/10 on SWE-bench-lite, 0 false confirmations.

Šta je zapravo open-source?What is actually open source?

Jezgro (L0, TSR adapteri, Evidence Gate, CLI/SDK) je Apache-2.0. Enterprise dodaje SSO/SCIM, multi-tenant, EU AI Act paket, ERP konektore i SLA. Nema „open-core zamke“: sve što je potrebno za produkciju jednog tima je u jezgru.The core (L0, TSR adapters, Evidence Gate, CLI/SDK) is Apache-2.0. Enterprise adds SSO/SCIM, multi-tenant, the EU AI Act pack, ERP connectors and SLA. No open-core trap: everything needed to run production for one team is in the core.

Radi li air-gapped?Does it work air-gapped?

Da. Nijedan dio ne traži spoljni servis; modeli mogu biti lokalni (Ollama/vLLM), a licence se validiraju offline potpisanim tokenom.Yes. No component requires an external service; models can be local (Ollama/vLLM) and licences validate offline with a signed token.

Koliko traje uvođenje?How long is onboarding?

Community: 15 minuta (pip/Docker). Pro: 1–2 dana uz naš pregled. Enterprise: 2–4 sedmice uključujući ERP konektor, SSO i dokazni paket.Community: 15 minutes (pip/Docker). Pro: 1–2 days with our review. Enterprise: 2–4 weeks including ERP connector, SSO and evidence pack.
