License

What you buy and how it is verified

The license is bound to a node and verified locally, with no “phone home” requirement.

Model

  • Community: Apache-2.0, no practical 1-node limit
  • Pro / Enterprise: commercial per-node license
  • Token: GAMA-XXXX-…, bound to licence_id, tier, node count, term and feature flags
  • Offline verification with a public key; no mandatory call to us
  • Expiry: 30-day grace in read-only mode — decisions are still recorded, mutating actions require approval

What we do not do

  • We do not delete your data when a license expires
  • We do not embed a kill switch
  • We do not obfuscate the code
  • We do not charge per API call, receipt or decision
  • We do not charge for exporting or reading your evidence after termination

Community → Pro is an in-place upgrade: same data folder, same database schema.

Open-core

Why the core is open

Evidence must be verifiable

If you cannot read the code that claims an action is safe, that is just trust again. The Apache-2.0 core can be audited and run offline.

No lock-in on evidence

Receipts are yours. Export and verification do not stop with a license, and the formats are documented.

You pay for production pain

SSO, multi-tenant, SIEM streaming, ERP connectors, air-gapped install and SLA — not access to the basic decision.

FAQ

Billing and license questions

Is Community really free?

Yes, Apache-2.0. No telemetry and no expiry. The production build with offline license, SSO and signed regulator export are Pro/Enterprise.

What does “per node” mean?

A node is one machine/VM running the Decision OS service in production. Dev, test and staging are free on Pro and Enterprise. Pricing is not per user.

Can we get an invoice instead of a card?

Yes. The first phase is invoice + manually issued license; online checkout follows. Write to info@gamai.io.

What happens when the license expires?

There is a 30-day grace period in read-only mode: decisions are still recorded, mutating actions require approval. We do not delete data or embed a kill switch.

Do you charge per API call or receipt?

Not for self-hosted Decision OS. The license is per node; receipts, decisions and evidence export are not charged per unit. Hosted SIGIL has a usage price of €0.002–0.02 per execution.

How are discounts applied?

Discounts are published and do not stack — the largest applies. Annual payment includes 2 months free; startups, academia and open-source maintainers have specific terms.
